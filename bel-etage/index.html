II — La Façade

Count two floors up from the black door.

The balcony with the roses spilling over the railing is hers.

In Haussmann's Paris, the second floor was the bel étage: the highest ceilings, the tallest windows, the only balcony that runs the full width of the house. The family who built the building kept it for themselves.

L'Adresse

Avenue de Breteuil, Paris 7e

A wide avenue of plane trees that runs straight at a gold dome.

The dome is Les Invalides. You saw it from her balcony, on the horizon behind the teapot. The house sits on the quiet side of the 7e: the Seine to the north, the Eiffel Tower to the west, and no reason to hurry towards either.

Nine colours, taken from the rooms.

IV — Les Pièces

Cinq pièces et un balcon

Five rooms and a balcony, in the order she would show them.

N° I

Le Salon

For long conversations

Slate walls, a bottle-green Chesterfield, a clock on the mantel that runs four minutes fast. The chandelier is lit in daylight.

Colour formula

Ardoise 45 · Bouteille 20 · Noyer 15 · Ivoire 15 · Laiton 5

Le choix

The walls went blue so the sofa would be the only green in the room. One green object reads as a decision; two would read as a theme.

Ce qu'on remarque

Two portraits of women above the fireplace. Neither is labelled.

N° II

La Salle à Manger

Dinner runs late here

A table for eight, two windows onto the boulevard, Bordeaux panelling that turns almost black after dark.

Colour formula

Bordeaux 40 · Noyer 30 · Vieux Rose 10 · Bouteille 10 · Ivoire 10

Le choix

The chairs don't match. Four moss, four rose. A full set would have looked like a restaurant.

Ce qu'on remarque

The chair at the head of the table is the only one with arms.

N° III

Le Cabinet

Her room

Bookshelves, a hand-painted crane, a brass desk lamp, hydrangeas the colour of the walls. The only room where nothing is green.

Colour formula

Lie de Vin 50 · Noyer 25 · Vieux Rose 15 · Laiton 10

Le choix

One painted panel between the shelves. The only pattern in the house that tells a story.

Ce qu'on remarque

The book on the desk is open, face down. Someone meant to come back to it.

N° IV

La Chambre

Through the half-open door

Deep green walls, ivory linen, a rose throw slipping off the bed. We are seeing this from the doorway, and that is as far as we go.

Colour formula

Bouteille 45 · Ivoire 25 · Noyer 15 · Vieux Rose 10 · Laiton 5

Le choix

The darkest walls in the flat for the one room that has to be dark. The ivory bed keeps it from feeling like a cave.

Ce qu'on remarque

Two lamps, both on. Two of everything on the bed.

N° V

La Cuisine

Where the day starts

Marble with violet veins, a copper pan on the range, dark plums in a bowl, a cane-back chair by the window for coffee.

Colour formula

Bordeaux 40 · Marbre 20 · Noyer 20 · Ivoire 10 · Laiton 10

Le choix

The dining room's Bordeaux carried into the kitchen. Cooking and hosting feel like one room split by a wall.

Ce qu'on remarque

The blue-and-white vase again. It is in every room. She collects them.

N° VI

Le Balcon

Five o'clock

Two chairs, a silver teapot, raspberries, the rooftops turning gold and a dome on the horizon.

Colour formula

Pierre 40 · Bouteille 20 · Laiton 15 · Fer forgé 15 · Vieux Rose 10

Le choix

Green velvet cushions outdoors. The house walks out onto the balcony with you.

Ce qu'on remarqueTwo cups. Hers, and one for whoever came to see the flat.
