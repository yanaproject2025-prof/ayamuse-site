01 · La Maison

Every house begins with a feeling.

RUBEDO begins with the red hour — that charged dusk when the garden deepens, the candles are lit, and a woman becomes entirely herself.

The name is borrowed from the alchemists: rubedo, the reddening, the final stage of becoming whole.

03 · The Emblem

One object you would know in the dark.

A single rose and a small golden key — the signature of RUBEDO. Every house needs one object a woman could recognise blindfolded. This is ours: the key to the red hour.

Beauty, sealed and kept.
